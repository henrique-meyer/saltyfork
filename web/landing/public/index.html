THE RECIPE FOR SALTYFORK

A good recipe is
just the beginning.

We’re building a place where recipes can grow with the people who cook them. Here’s what’s on the menu.

  1. 01

    Keep the good ones.

    Bring your favorite recipes into a personal recipe book, ready for the next “what’s for dinner?”

  2. 02

    Add your own twist.

    Fork a recipe, make it your own, and keep a link to the inspiration that started it.

  3. 03

    Pass something on.

    Share what you’ve made, give credit where it’s due, and inspire someone else’s next meal.
